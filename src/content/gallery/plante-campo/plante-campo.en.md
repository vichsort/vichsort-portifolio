---

The tests took place on three partner farms, with entry-level phones and no internet signal in the field. The model ran entirely on the device, in the [[plante|PlantE]] app.

The main lesson was about the interface: big buttons and instant feedback matter more than extra accuracy when someone is wearing gloves under strong sun.
