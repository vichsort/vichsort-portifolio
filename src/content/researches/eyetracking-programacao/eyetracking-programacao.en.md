---
title: Eye Tracking in Programming
institution: 15th Scientific Initiation Exhibition — IFC Campus Concórdia
description: A systematic review of how eye tracking is used to understand how students read and reason about code.
---

A systematic literature review on the use of eye tracking in programming education. The studies reviewed use the technique to see where students look while reading code, revealing attention patterns, problem-solving strategies and points of difficulty, with objective data on the cognitive effort each visual element demands. The conclusion is that the technology is still emerging in education, but has strong potential as a diagnostic tool for more student-centered teaching.
