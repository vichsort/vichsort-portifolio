---
title: How the Lack of Incentive and Teaching Affects Political Understanding in Brazil
institution: 15th Scientific Initiation Exhibition — IFC Campus Concórdia
description: A theoretical study of how the lack of political education and reliable information pushes young people away from democratic participation.
---

A theoretical study, based on a literature review, of the structural barriers to political understanding in Brazil. It shows how the lack of incentive for political education, limited access to reliable information and low-quality teaching reduce people's capacity for informed participation, leading to disengagement among young people and vulnerability to misinformation, which weakens democratic oversight. It concludes that encouraging political education in schools is a path to more critical citizens.

The research is the theoretical basis of [[faif|FAIF]], an app that gathers open federal government data in one place to make political information easier to access.
