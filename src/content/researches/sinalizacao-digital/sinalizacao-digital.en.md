title: "Digital Signage System: Accessibility and Sustainability in Internal Communication"
institution: 15th Scientific Initiation Exhibition — IFC Campus Concórdia
description: Digital signage for campus internal communication, reusing old monitors and TVs with Raspberry Pi and free software.
---

A proposal for a modern, accessible and sustainable digital signage system for internal communication at a federal school: schedules, announcements and events on screens around campus, with content managed centrally. Instead of buying new hardware, the system reuses old monitors and TVs connected to a Raspberry Pi, with free software such as Xibo and pySignage, in line with Brazil's electronics recycling law (Lei 14.479/22).

It's the research behind [[next-signage|Next Signage]], which also became [[sinalizacao-latinoware|a paper at Latinoware 2025]].
