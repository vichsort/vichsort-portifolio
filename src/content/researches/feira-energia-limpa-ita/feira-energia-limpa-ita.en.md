First place in the High School/Technical category of the 1st Science Fair: Energy Circuit, held by Consórcio Itá for the 25th anniversary of the Itá Hydroelectric Plant, with renewable energy as its theme. The final took place on October 28, 2025, at the Environmental Outreach Center in Itá, with 31 projects from schools in Santa Catarina and Rio Grande do Sul.

The project, presented by IFC Campus Concórdia, is a micro hydroelectric plant where water spins a turbine connected to a generator, with measurements shown live on [[energin|Energin]]. The first tests used a [[raspberry-pi|Raspberry Pi]]; at the fair, the roughly 30×40 cm model used an [[arduino|Arduino Uno]] with an infrared sensor to measure the turbine's speed. The group was supervised by Pablo Scariot.

Before the fair, the same plant went through IFC Campus Concórdia's [[2025-mic-ifc|15th Scientific Initiation Exhibition]] on September 23, 2025, as "Micro hydroelectric plants: your private property powered by clean energy", a feasibility analysis focused on construction and architecture, supervised by Guilherme Sperb.
