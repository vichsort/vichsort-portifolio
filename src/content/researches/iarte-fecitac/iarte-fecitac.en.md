---

An arts and culture paper presented at FECITAC 2024. Using an NVIDIA tool that turns drawings into ultra-realistic landscapes almost instantly, visitors drew live on a graphics tablet, and the group used the experience to discuss the technical processes, ethical questions and artistic implications of machine learning image generation. The underlying question: does artificial intelligence make art?

The full project, including the site that published the pieces live, is at [[iarte|IArte]].
