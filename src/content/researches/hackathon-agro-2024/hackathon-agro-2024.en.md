First place at the 1st Concórdia Ecosystem Agro Hackathon, held at IFC Campus Concórdia on September 20, 2024: twelve hours to build solutions for real challenges in the region's agribusiness.

The team's proposal, **AgroInsights**, combines rural property data with external data and produces reports with relevant information for every sector of the agribusiness chain, from producer to buyer. The same idea, business data side by side with the outside picture, came back a year later in [[prisma|Projeto Prisma]], this time for small businesses.
