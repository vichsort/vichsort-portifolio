A short paper published in the proceedings of the 22nd Latin American Congress on Free Software and Open Technologies (Latinoware 2025, Foz do Iguaçu), by the Brazilian Computer Society. It presents [[next-signage|Next Signage]], a sustainable digital signage system for academic settings that combines free software, Raspberry Pi and reused screens, cutting e-waste and cost while improving campus communication.

The paper describes the MVC architecture, with playlist management on a PHP server and a Python playback client on the Raspberry Pi, and explains why the group built its own platform after analyzing existing options such as piSignage and Chromium in kiosk mode.

The same research was presented, in a different format, at the [[sinalizacao-digital|15th Scientific Initiation Exhibition]] at IFC Campus Concórdia.
