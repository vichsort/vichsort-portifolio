title: The Tokamak and the Energy of the Future
institution: FECITAC 2023 /// IFC Campus Concórdia
description: A literature review on controlled nuclear fusion and the tokamak, the reactor that confines plasma with magnetic fields.
---

A literature review on nuclear fusion, the process stars use to produce energy, and the possibility of reproducing it in a controlled way on Earth. The paper explains the deuterium–tritium reaction, the most viable one here, and the tokamak, a torus-shaped reactor that holds plasma through magnetic confinement. It covers the technical challenges and advantages, such as high operational safety and little radioactive waste, and presents ITER in France, the largest scientific experiment under way, designed to reach 150 million degrees Celsius and produce 500 MW, ten times the energy it consumes.
