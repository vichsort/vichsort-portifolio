Finalist at the 1st IFC Campus Concórdia Hackathon, a week of development immersion and challenges from October 6 to 10, 2025, with students from the Internet Computing technical program.

The project brought to the hackathon was [[prisma|Projeto Prisma]], a financial dashboard for small businesses that combines revenue and expenses with Brazilian Central Bank indicators and uses AI to suggest decisions.
