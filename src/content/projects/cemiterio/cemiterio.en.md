## About Caboclo Cemetery

A cultural impact project dedicated to digitizing historical records, biographies, and interactive spatial maps for the preservation of Caboclo Cemetery's heritage.

### Features

- **Interactive GIS Mapping**: Coordinate navigation and burial site discovery.
- **Digital Photo Archive**: High-resolution preservation of headstones and historical transcripts.
- **Universal Accessibility**: Optimized for low-bandwidth mobile networks and semantic screen readers.
